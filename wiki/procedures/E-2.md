# E-2 — Faulted Steam Generator Isolation

Entered from [[E-0]] when one steam generator shows rapidly dropping
pressure or high steam line radiation, indicating a faulted SG (steam
line break or feedwater line break).

## Step 1 [id: identify-faulted-sg]
Check: which SG has decreasing pressure / high radiation / level decreasing uncontrolled
- Identified → #isolate-faulted-sg
- Cannot identify → [[ES-0.0]]

## Step 2 [id: isolate-faulted-sg]
Action: close MSIV and feedwater isolation on the faulted SG
Caution: do not isolate the only available heat sink without confirming intact SG availability
- Isolated, intact SG(s) available → #check-rcs-cooldown
- No intact SG available → [[FR-H.1]]

## Step 3 [id: check-rcs-cooldown]
Check: RCS cooldown rate within Tech Spec limits using intact SGs
- Within limits → [[ES-0.1]]
- Excessive cooldown → [[FR-P.1]]
- LOCA also indicated → [[E-1]]
