# E-3 — Steam Generator Tube Rupture

Entered from [[E-0]] when an SG shows rising level uncontrolled or high
secondary radiation, indicating an RCS-to-secondary leak through ruptured
SG tubes.

## Step 1 [id: identify-ruptured-sg]
Check: which SG has rising level / high secondary-side radiation
- Identified → #isolate-ruptured-sg
- Cannot identify → [[ES-0.0]]

## Step 2 [id: isolate-ruptured-sg]
Action: isolate ruptured SG (close MSIV, blowdown isolation, sample line isolation)
- Isolated, pressurizer pressure controllable → #initiate-cooldown
- Pressurizer pressure not controllable → [[ECA-3.3]]
- Loss of RCS inventory ongoing → [[ECA-3.1]]

## Step 3 [id: initiate-cooldown]
Action: begin cooldown using intact SG(s) to depressurize RCS below ruptured SG pressure
Within: 30 minutes of rupture identification
- Cooldown progressing, RCS subcooled → [[ES-3.1]]
- Saturated conditions developing → [[ES-3.2]]
- Cooldown via steam dump only → [[ES-3.3]]
- Inadequate intact SGs for cooldown → [[FR-H.1]]
