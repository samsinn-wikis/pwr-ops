# E-0 — Reactor Trip or Safety Injection

Entry procedure for any reactor trip or safety injection actuation.
Performs initial verification of automatic actions, then diagnoses plant
conditions to transition to the appropriate event-specific EOP.

CSF: subcriticality
CSF: core-cooling
CSF: heat-sink
CSF: rcs-integrity
CSF: containment
CSF: rcs-inventory

## Step 1 [id: verify-reactor-trip]
Check: reactor trip breakers OPEN AND rod bottom lights LIT AND neutron flux decreasing
Caution: confirm subcriticality before any other action
- Verified → #verify-turbine-trip
  Because: rapid neutron flux decrease confirms core shutdown
- Not verified → [[FR-S.1]]
  Because: must establish subcriticality before continuing diagnostic flow; manually open trip breakers en route

## Step 2 [id: verify-turbine-trip]
Check: all turbine stop valves CLOSED
Action: if not closed, manually trip turbine and verify stop valves close
- Verified or manually tripped → #verify-ac-buses

## Step 3 [id: verify-ac-buses]
Check: at least one AC emergency bus energized from offsite or onsite source
- Verified → #check-si-actuated
- Not verified → [[ECA-0.0]]
  Because: loss of all AC requires immediate transition to ECA-0.0

## Step 4 [id: check-si-actuated]
Check: safety injection actuation signal present
Action: if not actuated but required by symptoms, manually actuate SI
- SI actuated (auto or manual) → #verify-si-flow
- SI not actuated AND not required → #check-rcs-conditions

## Step 5 [id: verify-si-flow]
Check: SI pumps running AND boric acid flow to RCS established
- Verified → #verify-containment-isolation
- Not verified → [[FR-C.1]]
  Because: failure of SI flow with SI demanded is inadequate core cooling

## Step 6 [id: verify-containment-isolation]
Check: containment isolation Phase A complete (containment ventilation isolated, sample lines closed)
Action: if not complete, manually initiate Phase A isolation
- Verified or manually initiated → #verify-feedwater-isolation

## Step 7 [id: verify-feedwater-isolation]
Check: main feedwater isolation valves CLOSED AND auxiliary feedwater flow established
Action: if not isolated, manually isolate main feedwater and start AFW pumps
- Feedwater isolated, AFW flowing → #check-rcs-conditions
- No AFW flow → [[FR-H.1]]
  Because: loss of secondary heat sink requires bleed-and-feed

## Step 8 [id: check-rcs-conditions]
Check: RCS pressure, temperature, and pressurizer level trends
Note: this is the central diagnostic step — symptoms here determine the next procedure
- Pressure stable, no LOCA indications, no SGTR indications → [[ES-0.1]]
  Because: stable plant conditions allow normal post-trip recovery
- RCS pressure decreasing AND containment pressure rising → [[E-1]]
  Because: LOCA symptoms (inventory loss to containment)
- One steam generator pressure dropping rapidly OR high steam line radiation → [[E-2]]
  Because: faulted steam generator (steam line break or feedwater line break inside containment)
- Steam generator level rising uncontrolled OR steam generator radiation high → [[E-3]]
  Because: steam generator tube rupture (RCS-to-secondary leak)
- Cannot diagnose with confidence → [[ES-0.0]]
  Because: rediagnosis procedure provides expanded symptom evaluation
