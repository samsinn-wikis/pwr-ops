# E-1 — Loss of Reactor or Secondary Coolant

Entered from [[E-0]] when LOCA symptoms are diagnosed (decreasing RCS
pressure with rising containment pressure, sump level, or activity).
Maintains adequate core cooling via continued SI, manages RCS pressure
to optimize SI flow, and prepares the plant for cooldown and transfer
to long-term recirculation cooling.

## Step 1 [id: verify-loca-symptoms]
Check: RCS pressure decreasing AND containment pressure rising OR containment sump level rising OR containment radiation rising
- [Continue] LOCA confirmed → #verify-si-active
- [Recover] Symptoms ambiguous → [[ES-0.0]]
  Because: rediagnosis required when LOCA indicators are not consistent

## Step 2 [id: verify-si-active]
Check: SI signal active AND high-head SI pumps running
Action: if SI not active and required, manually actuate SI
- [Continue] SI active, pumps running → #check-rcp-status
- [Escalate] SI cannot be actuated → [[FR-C.1]]
  Because: ECCS unavailability with LOCA in progress is inadequate core cooling

## Step 3 [id: check-rcp-status]
Check: RCS pressure trend AND RCP status
Caution: tripping RCPs after voiding has begun may stop natural circulation; do not restart RCPs once voiding is established
Action: if RCS pressure < RCP-trip setpoint AND RCPs running, trip all RCPs
- [Continue] RCPs tripped per criterion or already tripped → #verify-msiv-status
  Because: preserves RCP integrity and avoids interrupting natural circulation later
- [Continue] RCS pressure ≥ RCP-trip setpoint → #verify-msiv-status

## Step 4 [id: verify-msiv-status]
Check: MSIVs status — closed if MSLI signal was generated
Action: if MSIV open with MSLI signal active, manually close
- [Continue] MSIVs closed (or MSLI not generated) → #monitor-sg-levels

## Step 5 [id: monitor-sg-levels]
Check: SG narrow-range levels and AFW flow to each SG
Action: maintain SG levels at no-load setpoint with AFW
- [Continue] Levels controllable on all SGs → #verify-ecc-alignment
- [Delegate] One SG level dropping uncontrollably with steam line break indications → [[E-2]]
- [Escalate] AFW flow lost to all SGs → [[FR-H.1]]

## Step 6 [id: verify-ecc-alignment]
Check: ECCS aligned in injection mode (high-head pumps suction from RWST, low-head pumps available, accumulators armed)
Action: if misaligned, correct alignment and verify flow
- [Continue] Alignment correct or corrected → #check-rcs-pressure-trend

## Step 7 [id: check-rcs-pressure-trend]
Check: RCS pressure trend after SI delivery
Note: pressure trend determines break size and recovery path
- [Continue] RCS pressure stable or recovering above subcooling threshold → #check-si-termination-criteria
- [Fallback] Pressure continuing to drop despite full SI → [[ECA-1.1]]
  Because: large break or ECCS recirculation failure mode
- [Fallback] Pressure decreasing AND containment pressure NOT rising → [[ECA-1.2]]
  Because: LOCA outside containment — leak path bypasses containment

## Step 8 [id: check-si-termination-criteria]
Check: all four SI termination criteria
Note: criteria are (1) subcooling ≥ minimum, (2) secondary heat sink available, (3) RCS pressure stable or rising, (4) pressurizer level on-scale or recovering
- [Recover] All four criteria met → [[ES-1.1]]
  Because: SI termination procedure restores normal RCS makeup
- [Continue] Criteria not met → #manage-rcs-pressure

## Step 9 [id: manage-rcs-pressure]
Action: maintain SI; control RCS pressure via charging and PORV operation as appropriate
Caution: do not interrupt SI flow before all four termination criteria are satisfied
- [Continue] Pressure stabilizing, criteria approaching → #monitor-rwst-level
- [Recover] Cooldown needed before recirc transfer → [[ES-1.2]]

## Step 10 [id: monitor-rwst-level]
Check: RWST level trend
Within: anticipate transfer to recirculation when RWST reaches low-low setpoint
- [Continue] RWST level adequate → #check-natural-circulation
- [Recover] RWST level approaching low-low → [[ES-1.3]]
  Because: transfer to cold-leg recirculation before RWST depletion

## Step 11 [id: check-natural-circulation]
Check: hot-leg / cold-leg ΔT and Tcold trends in each loop (only relevant if RCPs tripped)
- [Continue] Natural circulation indicated or RCPs running → #check-cold-leg-recirc-needed
- [Escalate] Natural circulation not established → [[FR-C.2]]
  Because: degraded core cooling if no flow path established

## Step 12 [id: check-cold-leg-recirc-needed]
Check: time since LOCA; cold-leg recirc transfer status
- [Continue] Recirc transfer not yet needed (RWST adequate) → #check-si-termination-criteria
- [Recover] Recirc transfer in progress → [[ES-1.3]]
- [Recover] Hot-leg recirc transfer time approaching (8-24h post-LOCA) → [[ES-1.4]]
  Because: prevent boric acid precipitation in core
