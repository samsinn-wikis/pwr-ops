# E-1 — Loss of Reactor or Secondary Coolant

Entered from [[E-0]] when LOCA symptoms (decreasing RCS pressure with
rising containment pressure) are diagnosed. Maintains core cooling and
manages SI flow until cooldown can begin.

## Step 1 [id: verify-loca-symptoms]
Check: RCS pressure decreasing AND containment pressure or sump level rising
- Confirmed → #verify-si-flow
- Not confirmed → [[ES-0.0]]

## Step 2 [id: verify-si-flow]
Check: SI pumps delivering flow to RCS at expected rates
- Verified → #check-rcs-pressure-trend
- Not verified → [[FR-C.1]]

## Step 3 [id: check-rcs-pressure-trend]
Check: RCS pressure trend after SI delivery
- Stable or recovering → #check-si-termination-criteria
- Continuing to drop → [[ECA-1.1]]
- Outside containment leak indications → [[ECA-1.2]]

## Step 4 [id: check-si-termination-criteria]
Check: subcooling margin adequate AND pressurizer level recovered AND RCS pressure stable
Action: if not met, continue SI and return to pressure trend reassessment
- All criteria met → [[ES-1.1]]
- Not met → #check-rcs-pressure-trend
- Cooldown needed before recirculation transfer → [[ES-1.2]]
